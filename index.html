Clayton Crossing is a small, gated townhome community in Oviedo with a community pool, zoned for Seminole County schools and close to the Cross Seminole Trail, SR-417 and UCF. It is a genuinely desirable address for renters.

We compared your home against every three-bedroom townhome rental in Clayton Crossing and neighboring Stratford Green — five listings, active and leased. Stratford Green homes all have two-car garages, and the Clayton Crossing listings include an end unit and a pond-view home, so we adjusted each one for those differences.

An interior unit without a view should sit below an end unit and a pond-view home. At $2,195 it does, by a margin a renter will notice.

It clears the $2,200 search threshold

Renters browse in price brackets, and $2,200 is a common ceiling. Both Clayton Crossing competitors are priced above it. At $2,195 your home appears in searches neither of them reaches — and is the least expensive way into this gated community.

It reflects the differences honestly

Your home is the same size as the end unit but doesn't have its extra windows and privacy, and it doesn't have the pond view down the street. Pricing it $70 below the end unit and $105 below the pond-view home is a fair reflection of that — and fair pricing is what makes a renter decide quickly.

It reaches more renters

The pond-view home and the largest Stratford Green listing both advertise no pets. Because we consider pets under our management, your home is open to renters those listings exclude.

It pays for itself quickly

Launching at $2,195 rather than the $2,275 June result costs $80 a month, or $960 across a 12-month lease. Every vacant day costs about $73. The lower price pays for itself if the home leases just 13 days sooner — and its two neighbors are already past 39 and 45 days.

Section Five

How we'll manage it

We will not promise a specific number of days — nobody can, and an invented number would only mislead you. What we will do is measure closely: inquiries, showings booked, and showings actually attended over the first five days.

If that activity is not strong enough for us to choose a well-qualified resident rather than accept whoever applies, we move to $2,125. If the market says that is still not enough, we go to $2,075 and keep reading it. $2,075 is a step, not a bottom — our commitment is to keep reacting to the market rather than defending a number chosen in advance.

We will also watch the two Clayton Crossing listings closely. If either one reduces again, we will tell you the same day, because staying below them is what keeps your home the first one renters call.
